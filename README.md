# Book Explained

Honest, spoiler-aware book reviews and video explainers — **[bookexplained.com](https://bookexplained.com)**

Book Explained publishes a short, opinionated review for every book it covers: a clear verdict up front, what the book is actually like, who it's for, and what to read next. Every book also gets a video on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@BookExplained).

## What you'll find

- **Reviews** – verdict in the first 30 seconds, no blurb rehash
- **Ending explained** – spoiler sections clearly separated from spoiler-free ones
- **Similar books** – what to read if you loved it
- **Series guides** – what to know before the next volume drops
